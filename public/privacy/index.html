Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Official Domain: https://mehandaarmy.net • Application Name: Mehanda Army Investments

Core Privacy Commitment: Mehanda Army Investments will never sell, rent, or trade your personal or Google user data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. All personal data collected is utilized solely to provide requested financial consulting, loan assistance, and secure account management.

1 Data Collection

Mehanda Army Investments collects personal contact and identification details solely when voluntarily submitted through our inquiry or account creation forms, loan applications, or OAuth identity integrations.

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, encrypted password, and phone number for SMS OTP verification.
  • Google OAuth Identity Data: When signing in with Google, we receive basic profile attributes (Google ID, primary email address, full name, and avatar image URL) as authorized by your Google Account permissions.
  • Financial Consultation & Loan Details: Information voluntarily provided for Agriculture Farm Loans (Dairy, Sheep, Goat) and Student Loan inquiries, including loan amount requested, purpose, and state/district location.
  • Identity Verification for KYC: Aadhaar and PAN details provided voluntarily by applicants strictly for statutory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) loan eligibility verification as mandated by Indian lending guidelines.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically for anti-fraud, rate limiting, and cybersecurity auditing.

2 Google API User Data & Limited Use Policy Disclosure

Our application, Mehanda Army Investments, uses Google OAuth 2.0 authentication to enable fast, secure, and seamless login for our clients.

Google Data Accessed & Scopes:

We request access exclusively to non-sensitive Google profile scopes:

  • .../auth/userinfo.email: To access your primary Google email address for account identification, login, and dispatch of transaction alerts.
  • .../auth/userinfo.profile: To read your name and public profile photo to display on your private Mehanda Army Investments dashboard.
  • openid: To verify your identity securely via an authenticated JSON Web Token (JWT).

Google Limited Use Compliance Statement:

Mehanda Army Investments' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Explicit Restrictions on Google User Data:

  • No Advertising Use: We NEVER use Google user data for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • No Sale to Third Parties: We NEVER sell, rent, trade, or monetize Google user data to data brokers, advertising agencies, or marketing companies under any circumstances.
  • No Generalized AI Training: Google user data is NEVER used to train machine learning or generalized AI models.
  • No Human Inspection: No humans at Mehanda Army Investments are permitted to read your private Google data, except: (a) when you explicitly grant written permission for technical troubleshooting, (b) for necessary cybersecurity audits, or (c) when strictly required by a court order or applicable law.

3 Usage of Information

Submitted information is used strictly to respond to specific financial consultations, maintain client accounts, provide agreed portfolio planning updates, process loan applications, and verify customer identities.

  • To establish, authenticate, and administer your private investment dashboard.
  • To verify loan eligibility and assess Farm/Student loan applications.
  • To send transactional receipts, 6-digit OTP verification codes, and security notifications.
  • To comply with regulatory standards established by Indian financial and IT statutes.

4 No Third-Party Sales & Sharing Restrictions

We do not sell, rent, or trade client personal information to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks.

Data is disclosed to third parties strictly under the following narrow circumstances:

  • Licensed Payment Gateways: When you initiate an online transaction, payment processing is executed via RBI-licensed partners (such as Razorpay and PhonePe) under strict banking confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where required by valid legal process, statutory audit, court summons, or regulatory authorities in India.

5 Security Protocols & Data Retention

Sensitive records are protected using standard 256-bit encryption and access-controlled databases.

  • Encryption in Transit & At Rest: All web traffic is strictly served over HTTPS with TLS 1.3 encryption. Backend database records and customer profile photos are encrypted using AES-256 storage standards.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database architecture enforces Row Level Security ensuring clients can access strictly their own records.
  • Retention Period: Personal account information is retained only for as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable tax, statutory, or financial record-keeping laws. Once the retention period lapses, data is securely erased.

6 User Rights, Account Deletion & Revoking Google Access

Every user of Mehanda Army Investments maintains complete ownership and rights over their personal information:

  • Right to Access and Update: You may review and update your name, phone number, and contact preferences directly inside your Client Dashboard at any time.
  • Right to Account Deletion (Permanent Erasure): You can request complete deletion of your account and personal records at any time. Simply use the in-app “Delete Account” setting or email our grievance team at info@mehandaarmy.net. We process verified account deletion requests within 30 days.
  • How to Revoke Google Account Access: You can revoke Mehanda Army Investments' access to your Google account at any moment through your Google Account Security Dashboard: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
